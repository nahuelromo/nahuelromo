# Hi, I'm Nahuel ⚡

I've been an electrician for 20 years. For the last 6, I've worked in wind turbine maintenance across Europe, specializing in high voltage, both onshore and offshore.

I've always been curious about how the different parts of the energy world work, why they fail and how they could work better. When I qualified as an electrician in 2006, it was hard to imagine technology evolving the way it has. That's why I'm learning data analysis and development every day, building real projects around electrical energy and the possibilities new technologies offer to improve it.

I don't consider myself an expert in anything, but I am curious and persistent. I like coming up with ideas, testing them and seeing if they work. And I believe technology makes sense when it helps people live a little better.

## Projects

- **[Predictive Maintenance for Wind Turbines](https://github.com/nahuelromo/wind-turbine-predictive-maintenance)** — Analysis of real SCADA data from 5 onshore turbines to detect failures before they happen. Python, scikit-learn.
- **[Wind Turbine Performance Audit — Turkey 2018](https://github.com/nahuelromo/turkey-wind-farm)** — Performance audit of a turbine using only basic operational data: energy produced, losses and their value in euros. SQL, BigQuery, Looker Studio.
- **Wind Farm SQL Audit** *(in progress)* — SQL analysis of 20 wind turbines in the UK, answering real questions from operations, maintenance and finance. PostgreSQL.
- **SunRadar** *(in progress)* — Web tool that calculates the solar potential of any rooftop.
